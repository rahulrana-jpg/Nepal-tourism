How the Escrow System Works

1

Create Booking

Buyer and provider agree on service details and create an escrow booking with payment amount, terms, and conditions.

2

Fund Escrow

Buyer deposits the agreed amount into the escrow account. Funds are held securely and cannot be accessed by either party.

3

Service Delivery

Provider delivers the agreed service (tour, trekking, activities) according to the booking terms and timeline.

4

Confirm Completion

Buyer confirms service completion and satisfaction. Both parties must confirm for funds to be released.

5

Release Payment

Once both parties confirm, funds are automatically released to the service provider. Transaction complete!

Benefits of Using Escrow

  • ✅ Security: Your payment is protected until service completion
  • ✅ Trust: Both parties are assured of fair transaction
  • ✅ Transparency: All parties can track booking status in real-time
  • ✅ Dispute Resolution: Neutral third-party mediation available
  • ✅ Automated: Smart rules ensure fair and timely payment release

Booking Status Guide

Pending

Funded

Completed

Service delivered and confirmed, payment released

Disputed

Issue raised, under mediation

Cancelled

Booking cancelled, refund processed if applicable
